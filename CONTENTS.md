# Contents

This repository contains lecture notes, tutorials, worked problems, Jupyter notebooks, PDF documents, mathematical visualizations, animations, and supporting material for YouTube videos. Each folder corresponds to a topic or teaching unit.

## Calculus and Analysis

- [`one_variable_calculus/`](one_variable_calculus/) — symbolic computation and introductory single-variable calculus.
- [`riemann_sum/`](riemann_sum/) — geometric and computational demonstrations of Riemann sums.
- [`vector_calculus/`](vector_calculus/) — parametric curves, vector motion, polar plots, and geometric algebra.
- [`differential_equations/`](differential_equations/) — symbolic and numerical examples involving differential equations.

## Algebra, Inequalities, and Visual Mathematics

- [`qm_am_gm_ah_inequality/`](qm_am_gm_ah_inequality/) — visual and computational material on arithmetic, geometric, and harmonic means.
- [`visual_proofs/`](visual_proofs/) — geometric and algebraic proofs presented through diagrams and animations.
- [`barnsley_fern/`](barnsley_fern/) — computational construction of the Barnsley fern fractal.

- [`plane_geometry_question_737/`](plane_geometry_question_737/) — notes and presentations for a plane-geometry problem involving arcs and a chain of tangent circles. [Video 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uVLGv0mEACs) · [Video 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XBjucUgcMCs).

- [`futurama_banach_tarski/`](futurama_banach_tarski/) — notes connecting a Futurama episode with the Banach--Tarski paradox and divergent series. [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zb1tEaJ2yFc).
